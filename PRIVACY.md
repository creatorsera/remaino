# Privacy

- The app has no internet permission, no analytics, no ads and no accounts.
- What you type is sent only to the app you are typing in.
- The keyboard stores numbers only: how many words you typed (today and in total) and how often common words are used, to improve suggestions. It never stores your messages.
- In password fields and fields that ask for no personalized learning, nothing is suggested, learned or counted.
- Data stays on your device. Android backups are turned off for this app.
