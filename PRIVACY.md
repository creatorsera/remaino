# Privacy

Remaino keeps what you type on your phone.

## What leaves the phone
- **Update check only.** The app has the internet permission for one thing: asking GitHub whether a newer release exists. It runs about once a week, or when you tap "Check for update". It sends no typing, clipboard or personal data.
- Nothing else uses the network. No analytics, no ads, no accounts.

## What is stored on the phone
- Typing stats are numbers only: words per day. Your messages are never saved.
- Learned words and next-word pairs are counts only, so suggestions improve. You can reset them in the app.
- Clipboard history (text and images) is encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. Passwords, private fields and items marked sensitive by Android are skipped. You choose how long unpinned items are kept.
- Backups are encrypted with a password you choose. A forgotten password cannot be recovered.
- Android backups are turned off for this app.

## Private fields
In password fields, fields that ask for no personalized learning, and apps you mark as sensitive, nothing is suggested, learned, counted or saved to the clipboard history.

## Optional permissions
- **Display over other apps** is only used if you turn on "At cursor" for the mascot or the floating keyboard. It draws a small window on screen. It does not read other apps.
- **Stickers and images** are shared with another app only when you send them. They go through the standard keyboard image feature, never through the network.
